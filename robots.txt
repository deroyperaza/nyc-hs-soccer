# Player pages aggregate PSAL's public box scores into a profile per named
# player, and most of those players are minors. The pages stay on the site and
# stay linkable; they are kept out of search results. A noindex header and meta
# tag back this up for crawlers that ignore robots.txt.
User-agent: *
Disallow: /player/

# produces is the card under the link in the chat. Blocking them did not keep a
# player page private -- the recipient has the link either way -- it just made
# the card say nothing. The noindex header still applies to everything they
# read, so none of it is listed anywhere.
